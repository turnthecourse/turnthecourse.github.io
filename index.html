ABOUT US

ABOUT

TURN THE COURSE is a post-metalcore band from Schweinfurt (Bavaria, Germany), founded in 2014. Their sound fuses raw aggression with melodic depth and cinematic atmosphere, shaping a modern approach to heavy music that is both powerful and emotionally resonant. With their singles Never Gone and Silhouette released in 2025, TURN THE COURSE opened a new chapter in their journey — heavier, more song-driven, and reflective — while already preparing further singles for 2026. These new tracks mark the latest step in an evolution that began with their debut EP No One Can Save Us (2018), which showcased the band's raw power and uncompromising intensity. In 2020 they joined forces with David "Dave" Beule (Ex-Vitja) and Pitchback Studios (Electric Callboy, Nasty, Any Given Day) to produce their second EP Grey Skies, blending aggressive metalcore with expansive soundscapes, technical precision, and deep emotionality. With the release of I Felt You Here (2024), TURN THE COURSE demonstrated matured songwriting, richer lyrical themes, and a refined balance between heaviness and atmosphere. On stage, TURN THE COURSE are relentless. Their shows are marked by visceral energy, moments of catharsis, and a powerful connection with the audience — whether in small clubs or on festival stages. Having shared the stage with bands such as Ghostkid, TSS, Skywalker, The Oklahoma Kid and more, they continue to prove that their music doesn't just work in the studio — it truly comes alive in front of a crowd.
